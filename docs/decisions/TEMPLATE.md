# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-....md)
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation forced a decision. Constraints that were real at the time — SolidWorks API limitations, performance requirements, what the code already looked like. Enough that someone who wasn't there can judge the decision on its merits.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly.

## Alternatives considered

Each option that was genuinely on the table, and the specific reason it lost. If an alternative was rejected on an assumption rather than a measurement, say so — that's the part most likely to turn out wrong.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, and what it makes harder or forecloses. Include the costs; a decision record listing only benefits isn't one.
